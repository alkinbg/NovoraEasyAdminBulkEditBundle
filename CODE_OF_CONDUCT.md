# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, professional environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback. Discuss code and ideas rather than attacking people. Respect different backgrounds, skill levels, and viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized comments or attention, and publication of another person's private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-related communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report violations privately to **alkinbg@gmail.com** with relevant context. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues. The maintainer may remove content, request changes, restrict participation, or ban contributors depending on severity and circumstances. Reports will be handled with discretion, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

This policy applies equally to maintainers and contributors.
